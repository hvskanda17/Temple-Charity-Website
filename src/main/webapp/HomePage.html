History of the Temple

We welcome you to the sacred abode of Sri Balaganapathi , located inside the HAL Quarters. During the construction of the HAL Airport, this masterpiece of craftsmanship was discovered and initially placed to display at the airport. However, because the airport being a secular place and closely associated with defence and military activities, the deity was later given a more befitting temple within the HAL Quarters. A small shelter was built first , which was eventually replaced by the present temple. The exact age and the architect of this sacred deity remain unknown, and details of its previous existence are lost to history, yet every visitor continues to experience a sense of divinity and bliss. Worship is performed twice daily and the temple attracts large numbers of devotees during festivals such as Ganesha Chaturthi. Sacred services like Panchamrita abhishekam (holy bathing), Hōma (fire sacrifice), special pūjās, and alankāram (adorning of the deity) are performed on festival days and on devotees’ special occasions. In addition, Sri Satyanārāyana Pūjā is performed on every Purnima (full-moon day), and the Sri Sañkashtahara Ganapathi Pūjā on every chaturthi of Krishna Paksha (fourth day of the waning moon). Sri Balaganapathi depicts Lord Ganesha in a child form, symbolising the power of new beginnings and growth; this deity is the ONLY STANDING BALAGANAPATHI in the entirity of Bangalore . worshipping this unique form is believed to remove obstacles in both spiritual and material journeys.The blessings of Sri Balaganapathi is also believed to bestow worthy children with high education and knowledge .In essence, this timeless deity remains an emblem of faith and wisdom, awaiting your visit to embrace the experience
